# Command: /geo-audit

Instruction recipe for an authorized read-only review, not an installed CLI command:
1. Inspect the selected installation’s dynamic `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt` and
   regional variants for publication, module, locale and market eligibility.
2. Contrast JSON-LD with visible content and the current Schema.org definitions.
   External validators require a separate recorded check; do not send private data.
3. Check OpenGraph, canonical, hreflang, sitemap partitions and timestamps against
   [public discovery](../../docs/GEO_PUBLIC_CONTRIBUTORS.md). Valid local output
   does not certify search indexing, AI citations or factual/legal accuracy.
